Invoice checklist

Tick every line before you send. Most unpaid-invoice months are caused by a missing line, not a missing client.

Before you send

  • [ ] Invoice number in YYYY-NNN form (2026-001), never reused.
  • [ ] From: your name (and entity, if you have one), plus a reply-to email you actually watch.
  • [ ] To: the right person — confirm the billing contact when there's an AP department; otherwise send to your day-to-day contact and Cc the payer.
  • [ ] Invoice date — today's date, in an unambiguous format.
  • [ ] Due date in words: "Payment due within 14 days of the invoice date" (or whatever Chapter 3's agreement said).
  • [ ] Line items described in the client's vocabulary, matching the deliverables in your agreement.
  • [ ] One total, bold and unmissable.
  • [ ] Payment method — link or bank details, with the payee name exactly matching the receiving account.
  • [ ] Attachment file name a human can find again: Invoice_2026-001_Client_Project.pdf.
  • [ ] Milestone label — which delivery this invoice is for, if part of a larger project.

The email around the invoice

  • [ ] Subject line: Invoice 2026-001 — Website redesign, final milestone.
  • [ ] One short paragraph: here's the invoice, here's what it's for, here's the due date.
  • [ ] One calm follow-up line about anything outstanding (e.g. signed agreement).
  • [ ] Optional kind line: testimonial ask (Chapter 6) only if it was promised, not as a surprise.
  • [ ] Send during business hours. Invoices sent at 11pm are opened late.

After you send

  • [ ] Log it in your tracker (spreadsheet or the companion invoice-tracker-webapp): number, client, amount, sent date, due date, status.
  • [ ] Calendar reminder for the day after the due date — for the friendly follow-up, not a threat.
  • [ ] Mark paid promptly and send a two-line thank-you. Paid clients remember freelancers who can close a loop warmly.

If it goes late

  • [ ] Day 1 late: one-sentence friendly reminder ("just noting this is now past due — happy to resend if it's been misrouted").
  • [ ] Day 7 late: same tone, one step firmer, attach the invoice again.
  • [ ] Day 10 late: pause work calmly in writing, exactly as your agreement said.
  • [ ] Day 30+: re-read Chapter 3's cancellation clause and decide if a more formal step is warranted for the amount involved. Keep every message; boring paper trails win.
