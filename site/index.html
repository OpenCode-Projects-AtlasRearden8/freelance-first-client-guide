The first-client survival kit: welcome

A plain-English manual for landing and surviving your first freelance client.

This guide is for the moment just before your first client — the part most freelancer advice skips. It covers the whole first-invoice arc in eight short chapters:

  1. Start here — the manual in five minutes
  2. The services one-pager — one page, one offer, one next step
  3. Quoting by project, not hour-fear — the calm math and the quote email
  4. Simple contracts without lawyer-fear — a one-page agreement, section by section
  5. Invoices that get paid — anatomy, rhythm, and follow-ups that stay kind
  6. Kind scope-guarding — three sentences to keep in your head
  7. Collecting testimonials — the delivery-day ask that actually works
  8. When an LLC makes sense — a tool, not a trophy (education only, never legal or tax advice)

Plus print-and-use companions:

  • Quote worksheet — fill this before sending any quote
  • Invoice checklist — tick every line before you hit send
  • Glossary — plain-English definitions of the words freelancing throws at you

Heads-up: This guide is education only — never legal, medical, or tax advice. Where business structures and contracts are discussed, a professional in your state is the right stop for your specific situation.

How this site is built

This site is generated from the Markdown chapters in the docs/ folder by a small local build script (python3 build.py). No frameworks, no tracking, no external requests — it runs entirely on your own machine, and you can read the entire pipeline in one file.
