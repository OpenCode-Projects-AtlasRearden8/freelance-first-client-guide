# The first-client survival kit: welcome

*A plain-English manual for landing and surviving your first freelance client.*

This guide is for the moment just before your first client — the part most freelancer advice skips. It covers the whole first-invoice arc in eight short chapters:

1. [Start here](00-start-here.md) — the manual in five minutes
2. [The services one-pager](01-services-one-pager.md) — one page, one offer, one next step
3. [Quoting by project, not hour-fear](02-quoting-projects.md) — the calm math and the quote email
4. [Simple contracts without lawyer-fear](03-simple-contracts.md) — a one-page agreement, section by section
5. [Invoices that get paid](04-invoices-that-get-paid.md) — anatomy, rhythm, and follow-ups that stay kind
6. [Kind scope-guarding](05-scope-guarding.md) — three sentences to keep in your head
7. [Collecting testimonials](06-testimonials.md) — the delivery-day ask that actually works
8. [When an LLC makes sense](07-when-llc-makes-sense.md) — a tool, not a trophy (education only, never legal or tax advice)

Plus print-and-use companions:

- [Quote worksheet](quote-worksheet.md) — fill this before sending any quote
- [Invoice checklist](invoice-checklist.md) — tick every line before you hit send
- [Glossary](glossary.md) — plain-English definitions of the words freelancing throws at you

:::disclaimer
This guide is education only — never legal, medical, or tax advice. Where business structures and contracts are discussed, a professional in your state is the right stop for your specific situation.
:::

## How this site is built

This site is generated from the Markdown chapters in the `docs/` folder by a small local build script (`python3 build.py`). No frameworks, no tracking, no external requests — it runs entirely on your own machine, and you can read the entire pipeline in one file.
